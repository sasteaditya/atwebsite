Privacy Policy

How Aditya Transport collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who we are

Aditya Transport ("we", "us", "our") is a road transport business based in Moshi, Pune, Maharashtra, India, operating since 2008. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use this website, request a quote, send an inquiry, register as a vendor or send us an RFQ, or pay us online.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, company name, phone number, email address, pickup and drop locations, material, weight, vehicle requirements and any message you write in our forms.
  • Business documents: vendor registration details or RFQ documents that you choose to send us.
  • Payment information: online payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We do not see or store your full card, UPI or net banking credentials. We receive only the payment status, amount, reference number and the details needed to issue an invoice.
  • Technical data: basic data such as browser type and device that your browser shares when you visit the site. We also store your light or dark theme choice in your browser.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry or quote request and to plan and carry out your transport booking.
  • To issue LRs, invoices and other documents required by law, including GST invoices.
  • To register you as a vendor or respond to your RFQ.
  • To process payments, refunds and to prevent fraud.
  • To improve this website and our services.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed: with drivers, vehicle owners and partner transporters who carry out your booking (limited to pickup, delivery and contact details), with our payment gateway and banks to process payments, with service providers that help us run the website, email and record keeping, and with government or legal authorities when the law requires.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website stores your theme preference in your browser. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent. You can clear stored data any time from your browser settings.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry, booking and billing records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet accounting, tax and legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw your consent to our using it, subject to records we must keep by law. Write to us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Third party links and logos

This website may link to other sites, which have their own privacy practices. Client logos shown on this site belong to their respective owners and appear only to show companies we have served.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact and grievances

For any question about this policy, write to us at info@adityatransport.com or contact Aditya Transport, Moshi, Pune 412105, Maharashtra, India.
